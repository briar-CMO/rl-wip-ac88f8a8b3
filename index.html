Open decisions for the week

  • The July target jumped to $68,990 (+33% on June), and week one is running about 13% under the pace needed. Efficiency is strong at 6.0x MER, so the move is to lean back into spend and chase volume, not hold the budget down - we have the room.
  • Mascara Innovation is live in email and was the best send of the week ($1,478). Line up matching paid creative while the angle is hot.
  • Scale the statics. Hair Ugly (7.4x), Before & After (7.3x), Spotlight (5.6x) and the Hair Mask static (5.4x) are our best ROAS at tiny spend. Brief more.
  • Refresh the fatigued winners, do not kill them. Viral UGC Mascara and Brows UGC have huge lifetime spend; recent dips are fatigue, not a dead angle.
  • The ‘prostaglandin-free’ search demand converts, but the current serums contain a prostaglandin analogue, so we cannot claim it. Hold that wedge for the Nov PGA-free Peptide Complex Plus; until then answer the objection with reassurance and proof.
  • Read-only Shopify access (Annemarie → BCUN) so NZ revenue and new-customer counts pull automatically instead of by hand.

✓ Where we are strong

  • Lash results-seeker: Social Proof + Guaranteed Results both scaling (Viral Mascara UGC, Before & After, doctor-developed proof).
  • Mascara / makeup lover: the Viral Mascara UGC is the top-spend ad in the account, pure social proof.
  • Gift shopper: the Free Gifts UGC returns ~4x and owns the GWP angle.
  • Hair thinning: the hair mask launch series gave this persona strong New-Launch coverage all June.

⚠ Where we are thin

  • Brow revivalist is only reached on offer / promo. RevitaBrow is 13x award-winning but under-shown on results.
  • Transformation (before / after) only really exists for lashes, not brows or hair.
  • Hair persona has no evergreen creative beyond the launch push, so it goes quiet once the launch cools.

✗ Gaps to fill (priority)

  • Ingredient-safety / reassurance is empty across every persona. The ‘prostaglandin-free’ search objection converts, but the current serums contain a prostaglandin analogue, so this is reassurance plus the Nov PGA-free Peptide Complex Plus story, never a ‘we are prostaglandin-free’ claim.
  • Mascara persona has no Guaranteed-Results or New-Launch creative (ready to seed the dark-brown mascara launch ~Sept).
  • Brow before / after transformation, and an evergreen Guaranteed-Results hair ad off the launch.
Compliance check: Keep claims testable and defensible. The current RevitaLash serums contain a prostaglandin analogue, so we never imply the existing range is ‘prostaglandin-free’ - only the Nov Peptide Complex Plus reformulation can carry that claim. “Guaranteed results” must lean on the trial / clinical backing, and we never imply RevitaLash treats medical lash loss.

What is winning

Two UGC workhorses carry the spend (Viral Mascara at $1,805 and Free Gifts at $1,176), both still profitable but fatiguing on a lifetime view. The real signal is the statics: Hair Ugly (7.4x), Before & After (7.3x), Spotlight (5.6x) and the new Hair Mask static (5.4x) are the most efficient creative in the account on a fraction of the budget. The angle mix is heavy on Social Proof and Promotional Offers; Guaranteed-Results statics are the under-funded winner.
