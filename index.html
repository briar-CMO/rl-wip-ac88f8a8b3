Three engines, three jobs

RevitaLash is not one business, it is three at different jobs. The creative should follow the job, not the product.

The harvest
Serums
Lash & brow

Hero range, 68 to 72% gross margin, brand-defended on search. The cash engine. Job: defend, optimise, refresh the fatigued UGC winners. Do not reinvent.

The attach
Makeup
Mascara · eyeliner · brow

$40 to 70 entry price. The easy first purchase and the AOV add-on. Job: gateway new customers in, cross-sell the serum buyer, lift the $150 basket.

The growth
Hair
Mask · shampoo · foam

New category, units doubled (~20 to 42), reaching a genuinely NEW customer. The $259 foam is a high-AOV monster. Job: expand the market. This is where the growth is.

Where AU lands, and the plateau

At this pace ABC lands about A$19,854 for July, well over the modest A$15,333 target, but roughly flat on the ~A$28 to 29k it has done every month since April. The target is set too low to be the goal. The real question, the one you are asking, is how to break the plateau.

Growing AU on Google alone. No Meta means no demand creation, so ABC keeps capturing the same existing brand demand each month, which is exactly why the line is flat. Three levers to break it, in order: (1) fix the Shopping feed (it returns ~10x but loses 62% of impressions to rank, a feed/bid ceiling not a budget one, so the cheapest growth is sitting right there); (2) test Google Demand Gen on YouTube and Discover (the only demand-creation tool Google has, the closest thing to the Meta prospecting AU is missing); (3) lean into the Asian-market demand already showing up in search (a Chinese-language “eyelash growth serum” term is converting) with a dedicated audience and creative.
